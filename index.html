📖 How it works

It is a word search, with a museum wrapped round it. Fourteen wings, eight rooms each, and every room you finish puts a real exhibit in your album with a fact on the back of it.

  • Drag across a word from its first letter to its last. Crooked is fine — the selection snaps to the nearest straight line. You can also tap the first letter, then tap the last.
  • Either direction counts. If you spot a word backwards, drag it backwards.
  • Every room hides one extra word that is not on the card — the curiosity. Nothing tells you what it is. Finding one is worth 🪙25, and it goes in your album. Find it before the last word on the card, because finding that one closes the room.
  • Stars: one for finding every word on the card, two for beating par, three for beating par comfortably without buying a hint.
  • Hints cost coins, and coins come from finishing rooms and finding curiosities. A hint always points at the word the grid is hiding best.
  • 📅 Daily is one grid a day, the same for the whole family. 🏅 Rush is three minutes and as many words as you can manage — that is the score on the family board.
